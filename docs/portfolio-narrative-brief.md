## 9T: one connected transformation

The user requested all three benefits: less repetitive analysis, trustworthy figures, and better decisions. Connect them in one workflow rather than presenting three unrelated dashboards.

The most defensible inspected chain is a statement's finding marker, a filtered finding, the account register and highlighted postings, and a reasoned review decision with an export. The demonstration should show how assembled evidence helps a manager decide what to investigate.

Important product boundary: **the reviewer verdict records a judgment; it does not rewrite or correct FINA's books.** A demonstration must not change underlying accounting numbers merely because a finding was reviewed.
